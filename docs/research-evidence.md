# Research evidence register

## Evidence present in the supplied project

The supplied material consisted of one HTML simulator. It contains synthetic display values and an illustrative software threshold. It did not include papers, official standards, field logs, laboratory reports, calibration certificates, ethics approvals, or deployment records. No research finding or field claim is made in this repository.

## Evidence required before technical or public claims

Before adding limits, algorithms, or health/safety claims, record the primary source and version, issuing organization, publication date, applicable jurisdiction, exact clause/table/page, units, sampling/analytical method, intended use, and review date. Verify the current official source and whether it applies to the specific water source, measurement method, population, and operating context. A threshold copied from a reference is not evidence that a prototype sensor meets that method.

Potential source categories for a qualified reviewer to obtain and assess include:

- current applicable Indian drinking-water standards and amendments from the issuing standards body;
- current World Health Organization drinking-water guidance;
- West Bengal and local water-operator procedures and applicable laboratory/sample-handling protocols;
- manufacturer datasheets, calibration manuals, and uncertainty specifications for the exact selected hardware;
- peer-reviewed validation studies for the specific instrument configuration and intended deployment.

These are source categories, not citations to findings or claims of compliance. No thresholds are adopted from them in this code.
