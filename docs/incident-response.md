# Demonstration incident workflow

## Scope

The interface simulates an illustrative turbidity trigger. It is not an incident-detection service and does not issue operational alerts. The `5 NTU` trigger and displayed samples are demo constants, not a universal limit or local authority instruction.

## Demonstration steps

1. Run the synthetic scenario or reset it to the synthetic baseline.
2. Treat the screen only as an interface demonstration.
3. In a real future deployment, independently verify instrument condition and readings using the selected equipment's approved procedure.
4. Collect and handle confirmatory samples under the applicable local protocol; use an appropriately authorised laboratory and suitable methods for relevant hazards.
5. Notify the responsible water operator/local authority and follow their instructions. Do not infer safety from this project's sensors or score.
6. Record a future real incident only in an approved operational system with appropriate privacy and access controls—not in this demo.

This document is not a substitute for emergency, public-health, laboratory, or regulatory guidance. The repository has no real incident history, escalation contacts, or operational response agreement.
