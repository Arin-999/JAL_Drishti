# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome good-faith questions and differing technical perspectives.
- Protect privacy and avoid posting personal data, credentials, or sensitive site information.
- Do not present synthetic information as real measurements or make unsupported public-health claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets, and knowingly misleading claims about water safety or field validation are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers or through an approved organizational contact. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. No maintainer contact or response SLA has yet been designated.
